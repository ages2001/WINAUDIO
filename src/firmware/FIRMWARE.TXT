WINDRV 1.0 - firmware files for the Intel audio DSPs and SoundWire codecs
==========================================================================

These files do not belong to WINDRV; they are distributed unchanged under the licences of
their makers, which allow redistribution (LICSOF.TXT, LICSST.TXT, LICCIRR.TXT).
WINAUDIO.SYS reads them from the WINDRVFW subdirectory of the driver's directory (else
from the directory itself), the Windows 95/98/ME driver from WINDOWS\SYSTEM\WINDRVFW.
For every platform the Intel-signed file is tried first; when the DSP rejects it, the
"community" signed file (name ending in C: Chromebooks) is tried.
